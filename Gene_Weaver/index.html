Algorithmic Architecture

  • One-Hot GEMM Matching: Encodes candidate k-mers into (N, 80) tensors; computes Hamming distances via matrix-vector multiplication.
  • MIT Specificity Score: Multiplicative distance and positional weighting (Hsu et al., 2013).
  • CFD Score: Cutting Frequency Determination across 16 RNA:DNA nucleotide pairs & PAM affinities (Doench et al., 2016).
